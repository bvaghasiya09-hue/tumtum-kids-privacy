Tumtum Kids – Privacy Policy

Last updated: 24 November 2026

Tumtum Kids ("the app") is a learning app for children aged 2 to 8, made by BVaghasiya ("we"). We designed it so that children can learn without anyone collecting their personal information. This policy explains what the app stores, what our service providers for advertising and crash reports receive, and the choices you have.

1. Information we collect

We do not collect any personal information. The app has no accounts, no sign-in and no chat, and it never asks for an email address, phone number, photo, contacts or location.

2. Information stored only on your device

To work, the app keeps the following on the device itself. It is never sent to us or to anyone else:

  • Settings, such as level, daily goal, sound, music, break reminder and learning reminder
  • An optional first name or nickname, used only for the greeting
  • Learning progress, stars, badges and days of learning
  • Pictures your child colours or draws, kept in the app's private storage

You can clear progress at any time in the Parent Zone (Settings → Reset progress), or delete everything by uninstalling the app or clearing its storage.

3. Advertising

The app is free and shows ads from Google AdMob. We have set every ad request up for a child audience:

  • Requests are tagged as child-directed (COPPA) and under the age of consent (GDPR). Google then serves only non-personalised ads and limits data processing.
  • Only ads rated G (suitable for general audiences, including families) are allowed.
  • The app does not use the Android advertising ID; the permission is removed from the app.
  • Ads appear only on menu screens, never during a learning activity or game. Full-screen ads are limited to a natural break after finished activities, and children who are new to the app do not see them.

To deliver and measure ads and to prevent fraud, the Google Mobile Ads SDK may process limited technical data, such as the IP address (used for approximate location and then discarded), device type and operating system, app interactions with ads, and diagnostic data. Google's handling of this data is described in its partner sites policy and its guidance on child-directed treatment.

4. Crash reports

To find and fix bugs, the app uses Firebase Crashlytics from Google. If the app crashes or hits an error, it sends a technical report: the error and where in the app's code it happened, the app's state at that moment (such as free memory and orientation), the device model and operating-system version, and a random installation identifier that lets us count how many devices are affected. It does not include your child's name, nickname, progress, pictures or any other personal information, and it is never used for advertising. Google Analytics is not used. Reports are encrypted in transit and kept by Firebase for 90 days. Google's handling of this data is described in Privacy and Security in Firebase.

5. Children's privacy

The app is directed at children. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children, and the app has no way to send such information to us. External links, the feedback email and the Parent Zone are behind a grown-ups-only question.

6. Permissions

The app uses internet access to load ads, to check Google Play for a newer version, and to send crash reports. Learning works fully offline. If a parent turns on the learning reminder, during first-time setup or later in the Parent Zone, the app asks for permission to show notifications; the reminder is scheduled on the device and nothing is sent anywhere. It does not request access to the camera, microphone, contacts, location or files.

7. Security

Learning data stays on the device, and Android protects the app's private storage. Ad traffic and crash reports are encrypted in transit.

8. Changes

If we change this policy, we will update this page and the date above. Important changes will also be noted in the app's release notes.
